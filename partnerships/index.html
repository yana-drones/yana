Partner with YANA

Bring drone-making, development workshops and professional-led learning to your institution. Start with a clear brief and an agreed plan.

For institutions and learning communities

We welcome enquiries from colleges, robotics clubs, makerspaces and organisations planning supervised student learning. A partnership can be a single event or a programme proposal shaped around your goals.

Tell us whether your priority is introducing components, working through assembly, exploring development tools or helping mentors support student projects.

Start a Partnership Enquiry ↗

Professional-led training

Discuss the expertise your programme needs. The proposed facilitator’s identity, relevant experience and availability will be provided for your review before a booking is confirmed.

Agree the responsibilities early

AreaQuestions to settle
LearningWho will attend and what should participants understand afterwards?
FacilitationWho leads the session, what is their relevant experience, and who supervises each activity?
EquipmentWhich drones, components, tools and computers are provided, and by whom?
VenueWho handles venue access, power, suitable workspace and any required permissions?
DocumentationWhat agenda, participant materials and follow-up support are included?
Commercial termsWhat are the price, schedule, responsibilities and agreed deliverables?

Send a partnership brief

Include your organisation, contact person, location, intended audience, proposed dates, expected group size and programme goals. We’ll discuss a practical scope before preparing a proposal.
