Hands-on drone workshops for students and teachers

Explore how drones are built and how their components work together. YANA welcomes students, teachers and institutions to discuss a hands-on learning workshop shaped around their group.

Bring drone learning into your classroom.

Students can explore the parts of a quadcopter and the relationships between its structure, electronics and control system. Teachers and institutions can discuss how these topics connect with their learning goals and participants’ experience.

We’ll agree the outline, practical activities, equipment and supervision with your institution before confirming arrangements.

Request a Workshop ↗

Proposed workshop outline

These potential topics are subject to discussion. The final outline will reflect your participants’ age group, prior experience, learning goals and available resources.

  • Drone components: identify the frame, motors, propellers, power system and flight controller, and discuss their roles.
  • Quadcopter basics: explore how the parts work together and introduce the ideas behind balance and control.
  • Assembly: discuss how the structure and components fit together, with any practical work agreed for the group.
  • Electronics: explore power and signal connections using suitable examples and supervised activities.
  • Flight safety: discuss responsible handling, supervision and the considerations involved in planning any drone activity.

The outline is a starting point for discussion. Any demonstrations or flight activities require separate agreement and are not confirmed by an enquiry.

Partner with YANA

Schools, colleges, academies and robotics clubs are welcome to partner with YANA to plan a workshop for their students and teachers. Tell us what your group would like to explore and what learning resources you already have.

Professional-led training can be discussed as part of the proposal. The facilitator’s relevant experience, availability and responsibilities will be shared for your review before arrangements are confirmed.
